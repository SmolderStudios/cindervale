Cindervale Idle · design review · build 0.9.122.6

Cooking never says what the food heals

Every one of the 19 cooking recipes makes a healing item, and the panel prints zero heal numbers. Cooked Salmon restores 270 HP; the card shows xp/hr, gp/hr, a tier badge and "Mastery 0". Three dishes heal over time instead of instantly and three carry a stat buff — none of that is on screen either. It is the same defect alchemy had before its rework, on the skill whose entire output is the thing it will not tell you about.

The fire has the mirror problem. It reports 4m 2s of fuel while 5h 46m more sits in the satchel, and the only way to see that is to open a modal.

01
No card states the heal. 16 dishes heal instantly, 3 heal over time (Marrow Broth 40 over 6s, Bone Marrow Stew 150 over 8s, Troll Stew 430 over 8s), and 3 carry a stat buff (Ratmeat Skewer +1 Str, Ogre Roast +2 Str, Infernal Roast +3 Str +3 Def for 90s). All of it lives in the satchel tooltip, none of it on the panel where you choose.
02
The heal ladder is not monotonic, and nothing shows it. Troll Stew at Lv 60 heals 430; Cooked Swordfish at Lv 62 heals 380. Drake Roast at Lv 72 heals 560; Cooked Shark at Lv 75 heals 520. Twice, the higher recipe makes the weaker meal — and on a ladder sorted by array order you will never see it.
03
The satchel's fuel is invisible until you open a modal. 640 pine + 410 oak + 180 ironbark + 92 ember + 40 frost + 11 shadow + 3 ancient = 20,751 burn-seconds. The bar says 4m 2s.
04
fmtDuration() stops at minutes. Load the satchel into the fire and the shipped bar reads 345m 51s. Live defect, one function, affects every caller with a duration over an hour.
05
"3 runs" does not say which thing runs out. Ingredients and fuel are two completely different errands — go fish, or go chop — and the card gives one number for both. The (fuel) hint only appears while the recipe is already running.
06
Two rows per card say nothing. "Mastery 0" is printed 19 times, and the output-per-hour line is ellipsised on 4 of 19 cards — "Cooked Minnow…/hr", "Bone Marrow Stew…/hr". That pair is what makes the card five rows tall.
07
Cook void eel renders out of order. Its data says Lv 90 but highFishUnlockLevel() returns 62, so the card shows "Lv 62" while sitting last in the array — after Cook shark (Lv 75), with the T19-of-19 tier pip. Data bug, not a layout one.

02

Four directions

All four draw the same rebuilt card, so the comparison is about the frame around it rather than about which mockup got the nicer card.
A · smallest change

Heal first

Nothing moves. The fire bar stays where it is, the grid stays a grid, and only the card is rebuilt: the heal takes the hero slot that xp/hr held, an instant / over 8s pill sits under the name, and the buff gets a chip of its own. The two rows that said nothing come off, which is what drops the card from five rows to three.

Four cards per row becomes three, because the shipped 262px column is narrower than "Bake Ship's Biscuit" and every long name was wrapping. The fire bar gains one number: 5h 46m in bag.

+1182px → 1055px, fits 1080p
+cheapest to ship — one render fn
+no new concepts to learn
−fuel still lives in a modal
−19 cards still one flat run
B · the fire is the skill

The hearth

The fire stops being a notification bar and becomes the object the panel is built around: a wide plate across the top with a drawn hearth on it, the burn clock at 28px, and the log shelf inline — every fuel you hold as a chip with a one-click load. The modal goes away entirely, and so does the trap: 4m 2s in the fire and satchel holds 5h 46m more sit side by side.

The plate takes one of three drawn states — burning, banked, cold — so the panel's mood tracks the fire instead of only its border colour. Below two minutes it drops to the ember plate before it goes out.

+kills the fuel modal
+the fire finally looks like the gate it is
+1182px → 1165px even with the plate
−+41KB of art in the file
−most new code of the four
C · one ladder becomes three

Two larders

Eight recipes are fed by Fishing, ten by combat drops, one by the sea, and they are shuffled into a single run of nineteen. Sitting on 340 sardines and no ogre tusks, you read a wall of half-dead cards and cannot tell which errand fixes which half. Grouped, each heading answers it: From the water — refilled by Fishing — 7 of 8 cookable now.

Inside each group the sort is by heal, not by array order. That is what makes Troll Stew out-healing Cooked Swordfish visible at a glance instead of being something you would have to go and read item descriptions to learn.

+names the errand, not just the shortage
+heal order exposes the two inversions
+1182px → 1167px
−three headings cost ~90px of chrome
−fuel still lives in a modal
D · stock first

The larder

Nobody cooks for its own sake; you cook so there is food in the bag when the fight starts. So the panel opens with what is in the larder and what it is worth — 17.3k HP across 116 meals — and the recipes below are framed as topping it up. Same move the alchemy cabinet made: the thing you own leads, and crafting is the refill underneath.

Only the dishes you can actually cook get cards, best heal first. The rest collapse to a strip that names what each is waiting on — a level, or an ingredient. The fire moves into the larder header, because the fire is the thing stopping the larder from refilling, not a separate announcement above it.

+answers "have I got enough food"
+dead recipes stop eating the panel
+1182px → 1158px, 16 cards not 19
−the ladder is no longer browsable in order
−biggest departure from every other skill
03

The hearth, generated

Z-Image-Turbo locally, 640×544, fire as the only light source, edges feathered to transparent so the plate has no seam against the panel.

The fire already has three states in code — burning, stored, out — and today they are distinguished by a border colour and an emoji. Three plates, one per state, cost 41KB packed as WebP: about 1.2% on a 3.4MB file, which is inside the wrapper's 6-second fetch budget with room to spare.

04

What I need from you

The card is settled; the frame is the open question.
Which frame — A, B, C or D? They are not exclusive. The rebuilt card is in all four, B's hearth bolts onto C or D unchanged, and C's grouping works inside D's "cook to refill" block. A likely answer is "B's hearth + C's grouping" or "B + D".
Does the drawn hearth ship, or stay a mockup? 41KB is affordable but it is the first raster art in the panel set. If yes, the same three-state treatment is the obvious next thing for the smithing forge and the firemaking bonfire.
Is HP/hr worth a row? Cooking's real throughput number — Cooked Shark is 134k HP/hr, Cooked Minnow is 22.8k. It did not fit on the card at three-up without wrapping a row, so it is off. It could go in a group header, or the card could go back to four-up and drop the ingredient chips.
Fix the two heal inversions, or leave them? Troll Stew (Lv 60, 430) beats Cooked Swordfish (Lv 62, 380), and Drake Roast (Lv 72, 560) beats Cooked Shark (Lv 75, 520). Arguably fine — the fish are cheaper and the stews are heal-over-time — but sorting by heal is about to make it obvious.
Ship the two loose bugs regardless? fmtDuration() never rolls to hours, and Cook void eel renders at the bottom of the ladder with a Lv 62 badge and a T19 tier pip. Both are one-line fixes independent of which frame wins.
