[h1]Ranged, Fletching, and a new Mastery column[/h1]

[b]Two new skills, plus two fixes from last patch that did not work.[/b]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]FIXES FROM LAST PATCH THAT DID NOT WORK[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]The farmhand still would not plant.[/b] Plant All was fixed, but the
farmhand was not. If you picked a specific seed for him he planted nothing.
Auto mode worked. Fixed, and the Journeyman Farmer replants again too.
[*][b]The consort upgrade took your materials and did nothing.[/b] The
purchase went through, the materials were spent, and the upgrade was undone
next time you loaded the game. Fixed properly.
[/list]

[b]If you lost materials to the consort bug, file a bug report in game and
they will be sent back to you.[/b] There is no way to spot an affected save
automatically, so it has to be done by hand.

[b]The dev can send you items now.[/b] A reply to your bug report can arrive
with materials attached.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]RANGED COMBAT[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Bows and crossbows.[/b] Bows are fast. Crossbows are slow and hit
harder, and you can hold a shield with one.
[*][b]Ammunition matters.[/b] Arrows and bolts carry their own strength, so
part of your damage comes from what you are firing. Eight tiers of each.
[*][b]Ranged uses one stat[/b] where melee uses two, so it gives more per
level to keep the two even.
[*][b]Heavy armour cuts your ranged damage.[/b] Leather does not. A
crossbow's shield does not count against you.
[*][b]A quiver slot[/b] on the paper doll and in the combat gear row, above
the shield.
[*][b]Three combat styles[/b] when a bow is equipped, replacing Attack,
Strength and Defence:
[/list]

[code]
  Accurate    no bonus, no penalty
  Rapid       fires faster, misses more, most damage
  Longrange   slower, and half the XP goes to Defence
[/code]

Rapid does the most damage, but its lead shrinks against tougher enemies.
Longrange is the only way to train Defence with a bow.

[h2]FLETCHING[/h2]

The supply chain for ranged. It uses three other skills.

[list]
[*][b]Shafts[/b] from any log. Better logs give more per log.
[*][b]Bowstring[/b] from flax, new to Farming and Foraging.
[*][b]Feathers[/b] drop from stags, lynxes, wolves, leopards and hares, and
come out of bird's nests.
[*][b]Arrowheads and bolt tips[/b] are made in Smithing.
[*][b]Arrows[/b] need a shaft, a feather and a head. [b]Bolts[/b] skip the
feather but use more metal.
[*][b]Bows and crossbows[/b] are built first, then strung.
[*][b]Salvager[/b] gives spent arrows back, up to 85% with the tree maxed.
[*][b]Category tabs[/b] on the activity list, so you are not scrolling
through 69 recipes.
[/list]

[b]Ten monsters drop ammunition.[/b] Anything with a bow drops arrows, and
armoured knights drop bolts. The tier matches the monster's level.

[h2]MARKSMAN[/h2]

A fourth Mastery column beside Onslaught, Bulwark and Warfare.

[list]
[*][b]11 nodes, 30 points[/b], same as the other columns. Draw Weight,
Fletcher's Thrift, Bodkin Points, Quick Nock, Heavy Draw, Point Blank,
Volley, Pinning Shot, Quiver Master and the Deadeye capstone.
[*][b]The board is a grid now.[/b] Discipline across the top, tier down the
side.
[*][b]All 50 nodes have painted art[/b], with a different frame for each
kind of node.
[*][b]132 points across 50 nodes[/b], up from 98 across 37.
[*][b]Combat level counts Ranged.[/b] Existing characters are unchanged.
[*][b]Every node description rewritten[/b] to be shorter and clearer.
[/list]

[h2]QUALITY OF LIFE[/h2]

[list]

[*][b]Keep the catch raw.[/b] A switch on the Fishing panel that turns off

[*][b]Radcliff's Tally.[/b] A keepsake for filing a bug report or an idea.

[/list]

[h2]BALANCE[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Marksman was too strong[/b] against melee once you spent your mastery
points. Six nodes were toned down. The columns are even now.
[*][b]No class favoured crossbows.[/b] They count as heavy weapons now, so
Rogue gets the dagger and bow, Berserker gets the hammer and crossbow.
[*][b]Smithing arrowheads gave more XP than the bar they are made from.[/b]
Lowered.
[*][b]Stringing a crossbow gave more XP than building one.[/b] Lowered.
[*][b]Arrows were worth less than the materials in them.[/b] More arrowheads
per bar, more arrows per batch, and prices adjusted to match.
[*][b]Melee damage nodes were also boosting bows.[/b] They now check what
you are holding.
[*][b]Attack speed bonuses were missing from the swing time shown to you.[/b]
Leather and Frenzy were both working, but the number on screen was wrong.
[/list]
