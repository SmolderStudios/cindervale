Guilds, but small

Three nouns instead of thirty. Everything below is built out of the same three terms, and every per-hour number on screen is real — a helper’s output is computed from your own best unlocked action in that skill, so it scales with your level, your tools and your tree without a second balance table to keep in sync.

Helper
Someone who works one gathering skill for you at a quarter of your own rate, while you do something else.
Slot
Somewhere to put a helper. You get more as your total level climbs — nothing to shop for.
Gold
What a helper costs to hire. No second currency, no reputation to bank and spend.

What came out of the earlier passes: seven guilds, a contract board, rank-up events, guild shops, a Patron slot, your own Lodge, standing and rivalries, guild expeditions, masterwork projects, reputation as a spendable currency, and item quality. None of it is here.

The smallest thing that still works
A

The Hiring Hall

panel 580px

One screen. A row of slots, one helper each, each working one gathering skill while you do something else. Hire with gold, change their skill with a dropdown, dismiss them. That is the entire feature — there is no second screen and nothing to come back and manage.

New words to learn helper slot

If guilds never get past this, it is still a complete thing rather than half of something. The two below are each this plus one idea.

A, plus a reason to come back
C

Helpers and One Job

panel 690px

A single standing request — not a board. One job at a time, chosen for you, refreshing weekly. Your helpers’ output counts toward it on its own, so it is something to check rather than something to manage. A board would be a list of things to compare, and comparing is a decision, which is the complexity this is trying not to have.

New words to learn helper slot job

The reward is gold and the occasional helper level, so it feeds the system already here instead of introducing something new to track. Only 110px taller than A.

A, plus what makes them guilds
B

Three Guilds

panel 1046px

The one thing that makes these guilds rather than a staffing agency: which guild a helper belongs to. Three guilds, each owning a group of skills, each with a rank that rises on its own as its helpers work. Every rank gives that guild’s skills a small bonus — yours as well as theirs, which is what stops it being a number that only helps the helpers.

New words to learn helper slot guild rank

Rank is a bar that fills itself — nothing to spend, nothing to choose. The cost is height: 1046px against A’s 580px, and three headings the other two do not pay for.

What I need from you

Four questions. The first is the only one that blocks building.

A, C or B — and they stack. C and B are each A plus one idea, so shipping A first and adding one later is a real path rather than a fallback. My pick is A now, C soon: the weekly job gives the system a heartbeat for very little surface area, where B’s three headings cost 460px for a bonus you never interact with.
Gathering only, or crafting too? Helpers are limited to Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Foraging and Farming on purpose — a helper who can run Smithing needs an ingredient supply, and that is a second system with its own screen. Gatherers just produce.
Do helpers work while the game is closed? Offline already exists as its own skill. Helpers running offline would either compete with it or stack on top of it, and that is a balance question rather than a layout one.
Where does it live? Its own button in the top bar beside Shop and Mastery. It is not a skill, so it does not belong in the left rail. The shots show it in the centre panel with the real rail and satchel either side, which is where it would sit.
