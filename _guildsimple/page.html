Guilds, but small

Three nouns instead of thirty. A helper is someone who works one gathering skill for you at a quarter of your own rate while you do something else; a slot is somewhere to put one; gold is what they cost. Everything below is built out of those three, and the numbers on screen are real — a helper's output is computed from your own best unlocked action in that skill, so it scales with your level, your tools and your tree without a second balance table.

What came out of the earlier passes: seven guilds, a contract board, rank-up events, guild shops, a Patron slot, your own Lodge, standing and rivalries, guild expeditions, masterwork projects, reputation as a spendable currency, and item quality. None of it is here.

What I need from you

A, B or C — and they stack. B and C are both just A plus one idea. A is shippable on its own, B adds a rank bar that fills itself, C adds a weekly job. Doing A first and adding one later is a real option, not a fallback.
Gathering only, or crafting too? Helpers are limited to Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Foraging and Farming on purpose — a helper who can run Smithing needs an ingredient supply, and that is a second system with its own screen. Gatherers just produce.
Does a helper work while the game is closed? Offline already exists as its own skill. Helpers running offline would either compete with it or stack on it, and that is a balance question, not a layout one.
Where does it live? Its own top-nav button beside Shop and Mastery is the obvious home — it is not a skill, so it does not belong in the left rail. The shots show it in the centre panel with the rail and satchel either side, which is where it would sit.
