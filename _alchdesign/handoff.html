There are no spare ingredients left in the game

I counted before writing this, and then checked the count. Every one of the 20 farming crops and all 14 foraged items is already an alchemy input. The sea is not free either: 15 of its materials are ship-build costs and most of the rest are troves, charts or jewellery. Exactly two items in the whole game have no consumer, and both are deliberately sell-only. So expansion has to come from new tiers, new effects, or new ingredients — not from mopping up waste, because there is none.

Correction to the brief. "Find out what herbs are being reused or not used" has an answer: none are unused, and the reuse is shallow rather than absent. Nine crops feed exactly one recipe each. Deepening those is the cheapest expansion available, and it needs no new items at all.

And a correction to my own first draft. I initially wrote that 42 sea materials were going spare. That was wrong — I had counted every item carrying sea:1 without checking what consumes them. Cross-referencing the hull, cannon, sail, rudder, hold and figurehead cost tables shows 15 are ship-build costs, and most of the remainder are troves, charts, sea jewellery or the pet and cape. The sea is not an open pool.

03

The sea is already spoken for

44 items carry sea:1. Alchemy uses two of them. That looked like a large open pool until I checked what else consumes them.

Sea materialCountWho consumes it
Ship-build costs15 salt_iron, krakenbone, tidewrought, cannon_barrel, driftwood, gull_egg, barnacle_iron, wrack_timber, tarred_rope, salvaged_powder, sailors_locket, spire_stone, siren_scale, voidwood, leviathan_scale — hulls, guns, sails, rudders, holds, figureheads
Alchemy already2 kelp_frond, fogweed
Troves, charts, jewellery, pet, cape~25 Not materials — they are collectables, keys and gear
No consumer at all2 drowned_coin, drowned_ledger — deliberately sell-only, the Admiralty bounty flavour. Not a gap to fill.

So sharing sea materials with alchemy means competing with the shipwright, not filling a hole. That may still be the right call — it would give Sailing a second reason to exist and Alchemy a late-game input pool it does not have. But it is a balance decision, not free real estate, and _sailChainAudit() exists to catch a hull that becomes unreachable because a reagent got eaten. Run it after any change here.

The alternative is new sea drops. Islands already roll bulk tables; adding two or three alchemy-only reagents to the deeper bands would give sea-fed brews without taking anything the shipwright needs. That is more work than reusing what exists, and cleaner.

A gold ladder is the obvious missing line. Skilling has Speed, XP and Yield potions; gpBoost is a first-class modifier everywhere else in the game (topaz gems, Fortune enchants, the Trove) and alchemy has no equivalent. Three tiers of a "Merchant's Draught" would slot straight in.

06

What I'd want decided first

  1. Do the missing tier IIs get written, or do the ladders stay two-rung? Everything else in this brief is bigger than that one, and it is a three-recipe job.
  2. Should sea materials feed alchemy at all? It gives Sailing a second reason to exist and Alchemy a late-game input pool, but it couples two skills that are currently independent — and Farming is the only supplier alchemy has today.
  3. Is a gold/sell-value potion line wanted? It is the one skilling modifier alchemy does not cover.
  4. Combat potions for crit / lifesteal / dodge? The stats exist and nothing feeds them from alchemy. This is the largest of the four in scope.
