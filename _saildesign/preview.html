The right side of Sailing

The chart is the best-looking thing in the game. The column beside it is where the feature actually gets used, and right now the Set sail button sits 1,134px down a 741px column — you scroll to sail, and scroll again to claim. Three ways out, at true size, plus a pass over the wording.

The scroll is a symptom. The column asks you to read a receipt, a travel brochure, a risk table and a reference manual before it offers you the one button you came for. Every option below starts by deciding what is a decision and what is reference, and only then worries about where things go.
01
The receipt outranks the decision. The last voyage's result card renders above the island card, so a good haul pushes Set sail further off screen than a bad one.
02
The odds legend restates the odds bar. Four lines of prose — "Triumphant · +40% cargo", "Wrecked · keep 30%, ship damaged" — under a bar that is already colour-coded and labelled. ~120px, read once.
03
Flavour occupies stat positions. "Conditions → Wrecker's Weather → Squalls that chew hulls apart." A full tile, no number in it, directly above the bar whose numbers it is describing.
04
Nothing signals which tab needs you. Only Admiralty gets a dot, and only for commissions. A ship laid up, a return waiting, a berth free — all silent.
05
The fleet strip is always there, rarely relevant. Two buttons and 60px at the top of every render, once a consort exists, whether or not either ship needs anything.

Option C

Dispatch

The column becomes a fast island list. Committing to a voyage opens a sheet over the chart with everything on one screen, and closes.

What I need from you

  1. Pick a layout — A, B, C, or a mix. A is a day; B is the real fix and a bigger job; C changes how sailing is played.
  2. The wording table is separable. Say yes to it and it ships regardless of which layout wins. Strike any line you disagree with — the nautical voice is deliberate in places and I may be sanding off something you want.
  3. Seaworthiness is the one rename with real reach — it appears in tooltips, the Shipyard and the odds explainer. Worth confirming before I touch it.
  4. Anything the mockups get wrong about how you actually play the panel. I staged these from a Sailing 62 save with a consort and one voyage back.
Mockups are static HTML at true column size — orders and drawer tabs respond, nothing else does. Palette and type match the live panel.
