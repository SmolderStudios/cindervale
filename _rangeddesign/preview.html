Cindervale Idle · design proposal · build 0.9.122.19

Ranged is half-wired and pointing the wrong way

The combat panel shows Ranged as a locked pill with a SOON badge. Underneath it, more exists than that suggests — and one piece of it is actively misleading. dmgType:'ranged' is already a real damage type that swings ±15% against a foe's weakness. weaponClass() already resolves it to light. A bow SVG generator already exists, and Sunpiercer already drops from Aureon at 120,000g with a description that promises this update.

But the Combat Mastery column whose internal key is ranged is Bulwark — HP, damage reduction, lifesteal. It has nothing to do with shooting anything. Zero of the 37 mastery nodes touch a bow. So the honest starting position is: the damage plumbing is there, the progression is not, and the naming will mislead whoever builds this if it isn't dealt with first.

01

The one decision that makes it not melee

If ranged is just a sword that reaches further, it is nineteen new items and no new game.
Pillar 1

The arrow is the strength stat

Melee splits accuracy and max hit across two levelled skills. Ranged splits them across two objects: the bow carries the accuracy, the ammunition carries the max hit.

A Runite Crossbow with Bronze Bolts hits like bronze. That single rule is the whole economy — it makes ammo tier matter as much as weapon tier, and it gives Smithing a permanent combat sink it has never had.

Pillar 2

Ammunition is spent

Every shot consumes one. This is the only consumable resource in Cindervale combat that isn't food, and it is what stops ranged being strictly better than melee: melee is free forever, ranged costs upkeep and buys reach and speed with it.

It is also the biggest risk in the whole design — see the note in § 06.

Pillar 3

You give up the shield — or the speed

Bows are two-handed, so the shield slot empties, exactly as twoHanded already does for Forgebreaker and Voidcleaver.

Crossbows are one-handed and keep the shield, and pay for it with a much slower swing. Thrown knives are one-handed and fast, and cap out low. Three real answers to one question.

How a shot resolves

QuantityMelee todayRanged proposalWhy
Accuracy Attack × 1.5  +  gear atk × 3.5 Ranged × 1.5  +  bow atk × 3.5 Identical slope. The bow replaces the sword; Ranged replaces Attack.
Max hit Strength × 1.6  +  gear str × 5.0 Ranged × 1.1  +  ammo str × 5.0 Shallower level slope — one skill feeds both halves, so the ammo has to make up the difference or ranged out-scales melee for free.
Defence Defence × 1.2 + gear def unchanged Ranged trains no defence. Hide armour is thin on purpose.

Damage type slash / stab / crush ranged — already exists Give armoured foes ranged resistance and flying foes ranged weakness. Wyvern Roost becomes the zone you want a bow for.

Net effect at Lv 99 with matched tier-7 kit: ranged accuracy lands on the melee line, max hit lands roughly 8% under it, and swing speed lands 10–25% either side depending on family. That is the intended shape — ranged should never be the strictly-correct answer, only the correct answer here.

The str column is the sword ladder again — 2 / 4 / 6 / 9 / 13 / 18 / 24 — so a matched bow and arrow sit exactly on the melee line, and every mismatch is a measurable loss you can read straight off the two cards. Flight Feather is the one genuinely new material: a common drop across the three hunt zones and Wyvern Roost, which is what the hunt zones are for.

The risk: an idle game where the gun runs out of bullets.

Cindervale's whole promise is that you set an activity and walk away. A resource that empties mid-session and silently ends combat is the same defect the fire had in Cooking — and it took a player report to find it there. Three rules, all borrowed from systems that already work:

Quiver. A twelfth body slot that holds one ammo type and does not consume a satchel slot, exactly like the seed vault. Auto-restock. When the quiver empties it pulls the same ammo from the satchel — the player is never asked to click. Say it out loud. The combat panel prints “340 arrows · ~48 min”, and the arena tile carries an ammo warning at 10% the way the hearth carries a fuel warning. Running dry retreats you from the zone with a toast rather than standing there swinging nothing.

Recovery closes the gap: 40% of arrows are recovered from the corpse by default, rising to 70% with the mastery node. Ammo is then a real cost you feel at tier changes, not an alarm clock.

Five pieces
Helm, chest, legs, gloves, boots — no shield, no cape. Same cat: shape as the metal sets, so the Crafting panel's tier grouping needs no new layout. Cape stays shared with melee.
Thin on def
Roughly 40% of the plate defence of the same tier. Hide buys accuracy, crit and dodge instead. The dodge and crit keys already exist in armorPerkBonuses() and flow through the single crit roll in combatTick — no new combat plumbing.
Plate penalty
−15% ranged accuracy per heavy piece worn, to a floor of −50%. Without this, the correct build is Starsteel plate plus a bow and the whole armour class is decorative. This is the single most important number in section 07.
Feeds itself
Hunt zones drop hides and flight feathers. Thornwood, Frostfang and Ashen Steppe stop being a side loop and become the ranged player's home — and hunting with a bow is the one thing they were always for.

09

What it plugs into

Five systems that already exist and get better rather than bigger.
The Quiet Hand
The second combat guild becomes the ranged guild. Ticket #58 asked whether the two combat guilds were one per style, and today they are not — The Iron Legion gates on Attack 15 and The Quiet Hand on Attack 35, so both are melee. Re-gate The Quiet Hand to Ranged 25 and the guess becomes right: Legion is the warrior hall, Quiet Hand is the assassins with bows, and Magic gets the third when it lands. Its quest pool switches to hides, feathers and fletching.
Combat Mastery
Wire ranged into the 28 unspent points, don't grow the tree. 16 of 37 nodes are in CMAST_UNWIRED and badged NOT YET ACTIVE. Ranged nodes go there — ammo recovery, a snare chance, damage to distant foes — so the 98-point invariant never moves and no save migration is needed. The column keys stay as layout slots; the misleading ranged name gets a comment, not a rename.
Slayer
Ranged-only tasks and a Fletcher's Cache in the reward shop. The shop already needs more sinks than the perks give it. A cache of tier-matched ammo is the cleanest one it will ever get.
Hunt zones
Thornwood, Frostfang, Ashen Steppe get a reason to exist past the pelt. +15% hide yield while a bow is equipped. Hunting with a bow is not a mechanic you have to explain to anybody.
Raids
Ranged-check encounters. A no-retreat gauntlet with a flying phase is the first raid mechanic that asks the party to bring a second damage type, and the RAIDS array is already data-driven enough to take one.

11

What I need you to decide

Everything above is reversible except these.
Does ammo get consumed at all? The alternative is a bow that needs no arrows and a Fletching skill that makes pure upgrades instead. Safer for an idle game, and it throws away the best economy sink in the design. My call: consume it, with recovery at 40% and a quiver that restocks itself.
One Ranged stat, or Ranged plus a second for max hit? Two stats mirror melee exactly and make the combat level formula trivial. One stat levels twice as fast and needs the shallower 1.1 slope in § 02 to stay fair. My call: one stat. Two is a lot of grind for a style that is already gear-hungry.
Is Fletching a thirteenth skill, or does Crafting absorb it? A skill means a 98-point tree, an icon, a panel, a guild slot and a rail entry. Crafting already makes every weapon in the game. My call: Crafting. The tree budget is better spent wiring Combat Mastery.
Does the plate penalty apply to existing saves? A player in full Starsteel who has never seen a bow is not affected. A player who picks one up mid-fight is. It needs a normalizeState default and a hint card, not silence. My call: yes, with a one-time hint the first time a bow is equipped.
Magic first instead? Magic is the other SOON pill and needs runes, a spellbook and an element system — more new machinery than ranged, which mostly reuses what's built. Ranged also has eight hides and a bow SVG already sitting idle. My call: ranged first, and let it prove the third-style pattern for magic.
